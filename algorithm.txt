Start.
Enter the current water tank level as a percentage.
Check whether the entered value is numeric.
If the value is not numeric, display an error message.
If the value is less than 0 or greater than 100, display an invalid water level message.
If the water level is less than or equal to 20%, display "Critical" and recommend refilling as soon as possible.
If the water level is greater than 20% but less than or equal to 50%, display "Low" and recommend refilling the tank soon.
If the water level is greater than 50% and less than or equal to 100%, display "Sufficient" and recommend continuing to monitor the water level.
Display the water level, status, and recommendation.
Ask whether the user wants to check another water level.
If yes, repeat the process.
If no, display a thank-you message.
END.
