COMMUNITY WATER SUPPLY MONITORING SYSTEM

A simple system for monitoring community water supply levels.

Project Description

This project helps communities monitor the amount of water available in their storage tanks. It identifies whether the water level is critical, low, or sufficient and provides a recommendation based on the level.

Objectives

•⁠  ⁠Monitor water levels in community storage tanks.
•⁠  ⁠Identify low water levels.
•⁠  ⁠Provide recommendations for refilling the tank.
•⁠  ⁠Help communities plan their water supply.
•⁠  ⁠Reduce unexpected water shortages through better monitoring.
